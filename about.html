Our purpose

Start with what
communities need.

Our mission is to build brighter futures through education and community action.

We are shaping our work around two connected foundations of well-being: access to health support and opportunities to learn. The details of each initiative will grow through listening, local knowledge and partnerships.

Our first featured program is book distribution. This site will become a clear home for the work, the people involved and the learning we can share.

How we want to work

Rooted in people,
responsive to place.

These are the principles guiding how we plan and grow our work.

01

Listen first

Understand local priorities, barriers and strengths before shaping an activity.

02

Make it accessible

Design around language, distance, culture and the daily realities of families.

03

Work with others

Build respectful relationships with communities and qualified local partners.

04

Share what is known

Communicate clearly and publish only information the Foundation can verify.
