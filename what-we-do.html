A considered approach

Listen, connect,
keep learning.

We want each step to reflect local priorities and make it easier for people to access useful support.

Listen locally

Learn from families, community leaders and local knowledge before deciding what is useful.

Shape together

Agree on practical priorities, appropriate language and ways to reach people.

Connect support

Work with trusted organizations and qualified providers where specialist support is needed.

Follow through

Check what happened, learn from feedback and share only confirmed results.

Program details, locations and results will be added as they are confirmed, so families and partners can rely on what is published.
