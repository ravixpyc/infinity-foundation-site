Developing priorities

Health support,
closer to home.

These are directions we are exploring. Specific services, partner organizations and locations will be published when confirmed.

01

Prevention and everyday health

Make practical health information and preventive support easier to access in community settings.

02

Women’s and children’s well-being

Listen to families and explore ways to make timely, respectful support more reachable.

03

Nutrition and early attention

Support awareness of nutrition and encourage early attention to concerns, with qualified guidance.

04

Referral and follow-through

Where possible, help people connect with appropriate health services and understand the next step.

A connected pathway

From first contact
to follow-up.

Health support is more useful when people know what to do next and can stay connected to care.

Hear the need

Start with community voices and local context.

Share useful information

Use accessible language and trusted local channels.

Connect with care

Work with qualified providers for health guidance and referrals.

Check what helped

Listen to feedback and improve the way support is offered.
